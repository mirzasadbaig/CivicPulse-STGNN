# Limitations and interpretation

- Persistence leads both 2026 windows in F1 and AP; no deployment or operational superiority is established.
- Geographic topology ranks first in AP among 20 additional full-node relabelings but two beat it in F1. Relabeling also changes category-aware alignment. Results are exploratory; no claim of formal geographic causality or a confirmatory permutation p-value.
- Five seeds characterize model initialization variability, not independent datasets. The paired date-block bootstrap compares spatial and identity only and conditions on the fitted seeds and observed dates. The 2026 windows overlap.
- The 2025 original test and 2026 outcomes were previously inspected. Future improvements need genuinely new data.
- April frozen-threshold F1 is about 0.034; retrospective best-threshold F1 is about 0.402 and AP is about 0.310. The retrospective threshold uses observed labels and is not a deployable score. This suggests both threshold sensitivity and weak ranking; no causal attribution.
- Historical GBM feature layout was invalid; the corrected layout plus validation-only search produces AP 0.5968 / 0.5417. Do not treat the original result as a fair comparator or attribute all improvement solely to the layout fix.
- Same-month year-over-year counts differ, but reporting behavior, weather, seasonality and classification changes cannot be causally separated. No proven crosswalk between old and maintenance labels.
- 80 selected nodes in one city cannot represent all demand. Targets are complaint count proxies, not independently confirmed crises. Community partitions describe only 21 dates in the older run.
- Draft manuscript, not peer reviewed or independently retrained. Author/mentor approval and code/data rights review before public release.
