# Supplementary material: descriptive community snapshots

The earlier, original single-seed 2025 model (not the controlled 2026 models) generated 21 learned-embedding-weighted Louvain partitions, dated 2025-12-09 through 2025-12-29. The archived diagnostics report six communities per snapshot, mean modularity approximately 0.619 and mean consecutive adjusted Rand index approximately 0.984. This is primarily a stability finding, not evidence of dynamic crisis propagation. The dashboard should show community assignments only for these 21 dates. The 731 daily observations are not 731 community snapshots. See `evidence/original_21_community_partitions.json`.

Method reference: V. D. Blondel et al., “Fast unfolding of communities in large networks,” J. Stat. Mech., P10008, 2008, doi: 10.1088/1742-5468/2008/10/P10008. This supplementary reference is inherited from the original manuscript and requires author review.
